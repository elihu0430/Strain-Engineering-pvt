Featured Project • January 2025

Gumbodzvana High School Solar Installation & Water Project

Location: Hwedza District, Zimbabwe • Client: Gumbodzvana High School (Head: Mr. Chirimo)

Project Overview & Engineering Scope

In January 2025, Strain Engineering completed a comprehensive solar power and water supply infrastructure project for Gumbodzvana High School in Hwedza. The project provides clean, reliable, and energy-independent water access for pupils, staff, and school operations.

  • Solar PV Ground Array: High-efficiency solar panels mounted on robust steel ground structures engineered for optimal solar irradiance and long-term durability.
  • Security Enclosure: Heavy-duty perimeter wire-mesh security fencing with anti-climb razor wire coils and lockable access gates protecting the solar generation asset.
  • Elevated Brick Water Storage Stand: Reinforced masonry structure with suspended load-bearing slab designed to support high-capacity JoJo water storage tanks.
  • Solar Pumping & Distribution Reticulation: Direct solar-powered submersible borehole pumping system with automated pressure controls and distribution pipework to school facilities.
