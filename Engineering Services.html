Engineering services

From viable plans to reliable infrastructure.

We plan, design and supervise civil, structural, water and environmental works that respond to real technical and community needs.

Capabilities

Integrated engineering for complex assignments.

Civil & structural works

Water treatment plants, reticulation systems, irrigation infrastructure, earth dams, roads, bridges, housing, steel fabrication and masonry works.

Groundwater & supply

Feasibility studies, borehole siting, drilling, casing, capacity testing, bore equipping, water-quality assessment and reticulation design.

Environmental services

Environmental audits and impact assessments, pollution remediation, water-quality monitoring, natural-resource management and wastewater advice.

Water & sanitation

Non-piped sanitation, solid-waste management, wastewater treatment, landfill assessment and sewer-treatment works.

Surveying & GIS

Topographical and engineering surveys, site layouts, non-title peg surveys and GIS consultancy services.

Project delivery

Project planning, contract management reviews, construction supervision, monitoring and evaluation support.

A considered approach

Engineering that protects resources as it performs.

Our specialists support clients from early feasibility and assessment through design, procurement and supervision. Environmental protection and the practical realities of operation are considered throughout the project lifecycle.

We work with municipalities, councils, mines, businesses and communities on assignments ranging from water supply and drainage to infrastructure and environmental compliance.
